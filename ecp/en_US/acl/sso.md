# Single Sign-On

Single sign-on (SSO) lets users log in to ECP with their company accounts. ECP supports OpenID Connect (OIDC) and SAML 2.0. This page uses Okta to show how to configure OIDC and how users sign in. For SAML, see [Configure SAML SSO](./ecp_login.md#configure-in-ecp).

## About Okta

Okta is an enterprise identity and access management platform. Administrators use it to manage employee accounts, application access, and multi-factor authentication. With this integration, Okta verifies the user’s company identity and returns them to ECP. Learn more on the [Okta website](https://www.okta.com/) and the [Okta Single Sign-On product page](https://www.okta.com/products/single-sign-on-workforce-identity/).

## Administrator Configuration

### Prerequisites

- Have an ECP system administrator account and an Okta administrator account with permission to manage applications and assign users.
- In ECP, configure the [Root Address](../system_admin/general_config.md#configure-root-address) under **System Settings** -> **General Settings**. Use the HTTPS address that users open to log in to ECP. The scheme, hostname, and port must match.
- Make sure the ECP server can reach your organization’s Okta domain over HTTPS. For production, confirm that API Access Management is enabled before using a custom authorization server. This guide uses `default` as an example. You can use a custom authorization server designated by your organization for ECP. See [Okta authorization server configuration](https://developer.okta.com/docs/guides/customize-authz-server/).

### Prepare Your Okta Organization

If your company already has an Okta organization, sign in with an administrator account for that organization. If you are setting up Okta for the first time, follow these steps to open an organization.

#### Set Up Your First Organization

1. Have your company name and the email address of the administrator responsible for setup ready. Use the [Okta contact page](https://www.okta.com/contact/) to work with sales on provisioning a production organization for enterprise SSO. To explore the Admin Console, you can [sign up for an Okta Integrator Free Plan](https://developer.okta.com/signup/) for non-production use. See [Okta organizations](https://developer.okta.com/docs/concepts/okta-organizations/) for the organization types.
2. After provisioning, note the organization’s sign-in address, such as `https://your-company.okta.com`. Follow the instructions you receive to activate the administrator account and set up the required authentication methods.
3. Open the organization’s sign-in address, sign in as an administrator, and click **Admin** in the upper-right corner to open the **Admin Console**.

#### Prepare Users and Groups

- In **Directory** -> **People**, check that the employees who need ECP access have active accounts and valid primary email addresses. For anyone who has yet to join the organization, click **Add Person** and have them complete activation according to your organization’s policy. See [Add users manually in Okta](https://help.okta.com/oie/en-us/content/topics/users-groups-profiles/usgp-add-users.htm).
- If you plan to assign the ECP app to a group, check membership in **Directory** -> **Groups**. After creating the app, grant access on its **Assignments** tab.

### 1. Get the ECP Sign-in Redirect URI

1. Log in as an ECP system administrator and open **System Settings** -> **General Settings** -> **SSO**.
2. Click **Add Identity Provider** and select **OpenID Connect** for **Protocol**.
3. Click the copy button beside **Sign-in redirect URI**. The address looks like `https://ecp.example.com/api/sso/oidc/callback`. Leave this page open while you obtain the Okta client credentials.

> **Screenshot to add** ECP Add Identity Provider dialog with OpenID Connect selected and the redirect URI and copy button highlighted. `./_assets/oidc-ecp-redirect-uri.png`

### 2. Create the Okta App and Assign Users

1. In the Okta Admin Console, go to **Applications and Resources** -> **Applications** and click **Create App Integration**. Some organizations must select **Classic experience** first.
2. Select **OIDC - OpenID Connect** as the sign-in method and **Web Application** as the application type, then click **Next**.
3. Enter a name, such as `ECP`. Configure the following options, then click **Save**.

   - **Client definition**: Select **Use Okta-generated client ID**. The creation wizard may label this option **Configure in Okta**. Use the **Client ID** generated by Okta when configuring ECP.
   - **Grant type**: Select **Authorization Code**.
   - **Sign-in redirect URIs**: Enter the full sign-in redirect URI copied from your ECP deployment.
   - **Controlled access**: Select **Skip group assignment for now**. Save the app first, then grant access to the ECP users or groups on the **Assignments** tab.

   See [Create an OIDC app integration in Okta](https://help.okta.com/oie/en-us/content/topics/apps/apps_app_integration_wizard_oidc.htm).
4. In the app's **General** tab, record the **Client ID** and **Client Secret**. Use **Client secret** for client authentication.
5. In the app's **Assignments** tab, click **Assign** and assign the users or groups that can sign in to ECP. See [Okta web app setup](https://developer.okta.com/docs/guides/sign-into-web-app-redirect/main/).

> **Screenshot to add** Okta Web Application settings showing the Authorization Code grant and sign-in redirect URI. `./_assets/oidc-okta-web-app.png`

> **Screenshot to add** Okta Assignments tab showing an assigned example user or group. `./_assets/oidc-okta-assignments.png`

### 3. Get the Okta Issuer URL

1. In the Okta Admin Console, go to **Security** -> **API** -> **Authorization Servers** and open the custom authorization server selected for ECP, such as **default**.
2. Copy the **Issuer URI** from **Settings**. For example, `https://example.okta.com/oauth2/default`.
3. In **Access Policies**, verify that a policy and rule apply to the ECP app. The rule must allow assigned users to use **Authorization Code** and let ECP request `openid`, `profile`, and `email`. If there is no matching policy, use **Add Policy** to create one for the ECP app. Then use **Add Rule** to add an authorization code rule for assigned users. See [Okta access policy configuration](https://developer.okta.com/docs/guides/customize-authz-server/).

> **Screenshot to add** Settings tab of the selected Okta authorization server with Issuer URI highlighted. `./_assets/oidc-okta-issuer.png`

> **Screenshot to add** Okta Access Policies rule showing Authorization Code and the assigned-user condition. `./_assets/oidc-okta-access-policy.png`

### 4. Save and Enable the Provider in ECP

1. Return to the **Add Identity Provider** dialog and complete the following fields.

   | Field | Setting |
   | --- | --- |
   | **Name** | The name shown on the ECP login page, for example `Okta sign-in`. |
   | **Issuer URL** | The **Issuer URI** you copied from the Okta authorization server. |
   | **Client ID** | The **Client ID** from the Okta app. |
   | **Client Secret** | The **Client Secret** from the Okta app. |
   | **Auto Bind IdP User** | Off by default. New users activate their accounts on first sign-in. When enabled, ECP uses the email from Okta to find an existing SSO account or creates one if there is no match. |

2. Turn on **Enabled** and click **Confirm**. The provider appears on the ECP login page.
3. Sign in at your deployed ECP login address with a company account authorized to use the app. Confirm that the account can access its assigned organizations and projects, then share the ECP login address and provider name with users.

> **Screenshot to add** Completed ECP OIDC form showing the Enabled and Auto Bind IdP User switches. Mask the client secret. `./_assets/oidc-ecp-provider.png`

:::tip
Users with an existing ECP password or invitation account should keep using their original sign-in method. If SSO reports that an email is already in use, contact an ECP administrator. An ECP administrator grants project access separately.
:::

### Manage a Configured Provider

- In the **SSO** list, click the edit icon to update the configuration. After rotating the Okta secret, enter the new value in **Client Secret** and save.
- To stop sign-ins through this provider, turn off **Enabled**. Existing ECP sessions continue until they expire.
- If **Bound Users** shows a user count, disable the provider to stop sign-ins. You can delete a provider only after it has no bound users.

### Check SSO Users and Grant Project Access

In ECP, go to **Administration** -> **Users** and find the user by email. The **SSO User** column shows the account type, and the **Status** column shows whether the account is active.

- **SSO users** sign in with their company identity provider. **Reset Password** is unavailable in ECP for these accounts. Reset Okta passwords through Okta.
- **Non-SSO users** sign in with their ECP email and password. An Okta account with the same email cannot sign in to that ECP account through SSO.

> **Screenshot to add** ECP user list showing the SSO User and Status columns and the unavailable Reset Password action for an SSO account. `./_assets/oidc-ecp-user-management.png`

When ECP creates an account on first SSO sign-in, it starts with no organization or project memberships and cannot access those resources. An administrator can grant access as follows.

1. In **Organizations**, edit the organization that owns the target project and add the user. See [Manage Organizations](../system_admin/org_management.md).
2. In **Projects**, find the target project and click the edit icon in the **Action** column.
3. Select the user in **Candidates**, assign the appropriate role in **Members**, and save. See [Edit Projects and Members](../system_admin/project_management.md#edit-projects-and-members).

## Sign in to ECP with Okta

1. Open the ECP login page and select the provider your administrator set up, for example **Okta sign-in**.

   > **Screenshot to add** ECP login page with the Okta sign-in option highlighted. `./_assets/oidc-ecp-login.png`

2. Sign in with your company account on the Okta page. If Okta prompts you for multi-factor authentication, follow the on-screen instructions.
3. The browser returns to ECP. If you see an activation page on your first sign-in, enter your username and any other required fields, then click **Confirm**. ECP fills in your email from Okta.

   > **Screenshot to add** ECP first-time SSO activation page showing a prefilled example email. Hide the activation link in the address bar. `./_assets/oidc-ecp-activate.png`

If you see no organizations or projects after signing in for the first time, ask your ECP administrator to grant project access.

After you log out of ECP, your Okta session may still be active. The next sign-in in the same browser may return you directly to ECP. To switch company accounts, sign out of Okta first or use a private browser window.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The Okta option is missing from the login page | Ask your ECP administrator to confirm that the identity provider is enabled. |
| Okta reports a redirect URI mismatch | Copy the **Sign-in redirect URI** again from ECP and compare it with **Sign-in redirect URIs** in Okta. Check the scheme, hostname, port, and path. |
| Okta reports that you cannot access the app | Ask your Okta administrator to check application assignments and the authorization server's access policy. |
| ECP reports that the sign-in has expired | Start again from the ECP login page and complete sign-in in the same browser. |
